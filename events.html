Access at our events

If something about a venue or format would make it hard to attend, tell us before the event and we will sort it. This is not a favour and you do not need to explain why.

Venues

Step-free

All termly sessions are in step-free rooms with accessible toilets. Symposium venues are checked before booking.

Networking

Meet people in the field

Every session leaves time to talk afterwards, with students from other courses and with speakers working in global health.

Cost

Nothing to pay

Branch events are free. Travel bursaries cover national conferences, no means test.

Comfort

Quiet space

A quiet room at all-day events, and it is completely fine to leave a session partway through.
