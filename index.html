Students for Global Health · University of Oxford

Global health is
a local issue.

We are the Oxford branch of the UK's largest student-led global health network — open to every subject, not just medics. We run a whole range of events from teaching, campaigns and conferences — sending students to the rooms where health decisions actually get made.

What we do

Four ways in.

You don't have to commit to all of it. Come along to one of our events and see which resonates with you the most!

Education

Teaching

Weekly sessions on the things the degree skips — health financing, climate and health, migration, the politics of drug pricing.

Advocacy

Campaigns

Briefings for MPs, motions through Oxford SU, and joint national campaigns on access to medicines and NHS workforce policy.

Events

Conferences

National conferences every year, with opportunities to attend continental and international conferences too.

International

Delegations

Oxford students have interacted with major institutions including the World Health Assembly and UK Parliament.

Get involved

There's a place for you here.

Membership is free for the year. It gets you on the mailing list, into every session, and first in line for conference bursaries and delegation places. You don't need any background in health — genuinely.

Take a look at the national committee Email the Oxford committee
  1. Sign up — one form, takes a minute. No fee.
  2. Come to anything — a session, the symposium, the pub afterwards.
  3. Take something on — run a campaign, chair a panel, join the committee.
  4. Go further — apply for a national role or an international delegation. We'll back you.
